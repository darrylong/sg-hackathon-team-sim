KEY FINDINGS
==============================================================================
- Last 4 quarters revenue (outlier excluded): $4,289.6M; AMS is the biggest region (58.2%).
- One EMEA order line in 2026Q1 inflates that region-quarter by 15% - it is excluded from all trends and forecasts.
- Strong Q3 seasonality: total revenue moved -2.8% (2024) and -5.2% (2025) from Q2 to Q3, so the 2026Q3 forecast must not simply copy 2026Q2.
- Target attainment by tier ranges from 89% (Silver) to 98% (Business).
- Revenue concentration: top 10 partners make 8.9-18.0% of each region's revenue.
- The partner base grew from 1,945 to 2,600 onboarded partners; new partners ramp up and must not be flagged just for low early revenue.
- Historical leaver rate: 0.3-1.7% of active partners per quarter (latest 2026Q2: 1.2%).
- 99% of past leavers were active again in a later quarter: in the history, 'leaving' is almost always a temporary gap, not permanent churn.
- Past leavers did NOT fade first - at Q-1 their revenue index was 167 (Q-4 = 100) vs 123 for stayers. What sets them apart is order frequency: median 2.8 order lines per quarter vs 8.4 for stayers - rare, lumpy buyers.
- Implication for the model: order frequency/regularity and recency are likely stronger risk signals than revenue trend; the 2026Q3 leavers may behave differently from these historical gaps, so validate with time-travel labels rather than assuming a fade.
